Working Principles

Set decision criteria and assign operational responsibilities before implementation

Project scope defines who makes decisions, what evidence is required, how results are reviewed, and who is responsible for operations.

01 DECISION

Define the decision to support

Identify who makes the decision, when it arises, what must be decided, and which business rules, data, and constraints apply.

02 EVIDENCE

Define the evidence and its limitations

Document the sources, calculation rules, data-quality conditions, and open questions for each analysis.

03 CONTROL

Set review and approval thresholds

Based on business impact and risk, define when a result should be reviewed or approved and when the process should stop.

04 OPERATION

Assign responsibility for operations

Define access rights, evaluation procedures, change control, incident response, and lines of accountability during implementation.

KOAA Methodology

Make business knowledge the basis for Agentic Analytics

KOAA is Nex & Bridge's methodology for organizing business concepts, rules, exceptions, and evidence as knowledge that can inform analysis. It applies that knowledge to agent workflows and validation criteria.

Make knowledge use traceable

Trace which knowledge was used at each stage and under which conditions. Validation requirements depend on business criticality, data conditions, and operational responsibility.
