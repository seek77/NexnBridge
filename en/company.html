From the knowledge foundation
to agents, applications, and operations

We treat enterprise knowledge and data, AI agents and business applications, and the controls required to operate them as one delivery scope.

Enterprise AI takes more than a model and a prompt

Business processes and decision criteria, the meaning and relationships in data, the role assigned to AI, and the controls around its use must be designed together. Ontologies, knowledge graphs, and semantic layers are ways to make that foundation usable by AI.
