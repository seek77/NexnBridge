Why This Matters

AI investment and day-to-day operations don't connect

Companies have models, data, and PoCs. The work itself hasn't changed. When knowledge, data, Agents, and operating rules are each managed as separate projects, PoC results don't carry over into real operations.

Knowledge

The reasons, exceptions, and sequence behind a judgment live in people and documents, not in any system.

Agents

Each PoC runs on its own assumptions and data, so results can't be reused or connected to real systems.

Operating Controls

Evaluation, access, and audit get discussed right before launch — which is what delays launch.

The problem isn't how good any one Agent is —
it's that there's no enterprise structure for it to work in.
