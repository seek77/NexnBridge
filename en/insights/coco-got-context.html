CoCo Got Context
From New Hire to Domain Expert

At first, CoCo felt like a smart new hire.

It could build dashboards, turn questions into charts, and help organize information on a screen.

Then we connected CoCo Desktop to our Knowledge Graph.

That is when things changed.

CoCo started moving beyond simple dashboard tasks.
It began following scenario-based analysis flows, understanding business context, and helping structure decision paths.

Not just
“What happened?”

But also
“What should we check first?”
“What is driving the issue?”
“Which scenarios should we compare?”

The real shift was not just better AI.
It was AI connected to enterprise knowledge.
