Privacy Policy

Nex & Bridge Co., Ltd. (the "Company") collects and uses personal data submitted through the contact form on this website. A business email address, combined with a name, can identify a specific individual and is treated as personal data under South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). This policy explains how we handle it.

1. What We Collect, and Why

Data collectedName, company, email address, area of interest, message
PurposeResponding to inquiries and scoping engagements
MethodWebsite contact form (sent directly via the visitor's own email client)
Retention1 year after the inquiry is resolved, then deleted without delay

2. Processing and Third-Party Sharing

This form doesn't run through a server or any third-party form-processing service. When you submit it, your own email application opens with the message pre-filled, and you send it yourself to contact@nexenbridge.com.

No personal data is entrusted to a third party or transferred outside your jurisdiction in this process. If our contact process later moves to a third-party form service, this policy will be updated in advance to name the processor and destination country.

3. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of, or a halt to processing of your personal data at any time. Email contact@nexenbridge.com and we'll act without delay.

4. Destruction

Personal data is destroyed without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled. Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery.
