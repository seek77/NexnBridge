Privacy Policy

Nex & Bridge Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) collects and uses personal information only when a visitor sends an inquiry from their own email app and the Company receives it in its mailbox. A business email address combined with a name can identify a specific individual and therefore constitutes personal information. This policy explains how that information is collected, used, retained, and destroyed.

1. Information We Collect and Why

Required informationName, company name, email address, and area of interest
Optional informationMessage
PurposeResponding to inquiries and conducting consultations
Collection methodThe visitor sends the inquiry from their own email app, and the Company receives it in its mailbox
Retention periodOne year after the inquiry has been handled; the information is then destroyed without delay

2. How the Inquiry Is Delivered

The website does not store information entered in the inquiry form on a separate server or with an external form-processing service. Selecting the inquiry button opens a draft message in the visitor’s own email app. The Company receives the inquiry only after the visitor selects Send and the message is delivered to contact@nexenbridge.com.

3. Your Rights

You may request access to your personal information or ask the Company to correct, delete, or stop processing it at any time. Email contact@nexenbridge.com. The Company will take action without delay.

4. Destruction Procedure

The Company destroys personal information without delay when the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been fulfilled. Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery or reproduction.
