Core Service Areas

Core Service Areas

Enterprise Knowledge Engineering

Turns scattered business concepts, rules, exceptions, and rationale into a structure Agents and people can both use — Business Ontology, Knowledge Graph, Decision Architecture. For companies where judgment criteria differ by person, or where RAG retrieves documents but still can't produce a consistent decision.

Lead Engagement Area

Snowflake Data & AI Services

Data foundation, SAP/legacy migration, modeling and Semantic Layer, AI & Agent activation — plus two entry points we recommend starting with:

  • Cost Optimization (FinOps) — Snowflake bills by usage; the fastest way to show measurable savings without touching business systems.
  • Access & Security Design — who can see what, and specifically, what an Agent is allowed to see on a user's behalf.

Agentic Solution Engineering

Domain-aware Agents, decision applications, simulation and optimisation, workflow automation, and execution handoff into ERP/MES/APS with results tracked back.

AgentOps & Managed Operations (cross-cutting)

Evaluation, guardrails, access boundaries, observability, and managed operations — designed in before deployment, not bolted on after.
