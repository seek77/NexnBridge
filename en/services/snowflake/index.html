AI-Ready Data Foundation

Design the data model around how the business uses the data

We analyze business documents, reports, SQL, and questions from users to identify business objects, relationships, metrics, and calculation rules. Domain experts review the results before they are incorporated into the Enterprise Data Model and Semantic Views.

The model is tested against real business questions and verified results before it is used as the foundation for analytics and AI.

Move SAP data and history with defined validation criteria

Data Engineering & Integration

Design pipelines around when and how the data is used

We consider when the business needs the data, source-system load, history, recovery, and consistency requirements before choosing Batch, CDC, or Streaming patterns.
