Where We Apply

Assess the work, business rules, and data first.
Then choose the delivery approach that fits.

We begin by asking how often the work recurs, how consequential the decision is, whether the business rules and data are ready, and whether the outcome can be measured.

Fit Criteria

Choose the approach that fits the way the work is performed

Nature of the WorkApproach to Consider
The procedure is fixed and exceptions are rareWorkflow or Rule Engine
The work is a recurring calculation with clear inputs and outputsProcedural Logic or Optimization Model
The questions and evidence required vary with the situationAgentic Analysis
A person must compare the impact and limitations of several options before decidingKOAA & Decision Brief

The choice depends on the work itself. A fixed workflow or calculation does not need to be turned into an agentic process.

KOAA & Decision Brief — When people need to compare the expected effects and limitations of several alternatives, KOAA structures business concepts, rules, exceptions, and evidence so they can guide the analysis workflow and its validation criteria. A Decision Brief gives human reviewers a structured view of the evidence, assumptions, constraints, expected impacts, and open questions.

A Common Decision Frame

Use a consistent decision framework across business domains

Decision to be made → Business criteria → Data evidence
→ Options and impact → Human decision → Execution outcome

The terminology and metrics change by domain. The design questions remain consistent: who makes the decision, what evidence is used, and how the result will be checked.
